Every other data-i18n string checked matches exactly; this one slipped. Masked for JS users by the intro overlay, but affects no-JS/SEO fallback and the 8s JS-failure safety path.

**[P3] Floating CTA duplicates the hero's own primary CTA before it's earned a reason to exist.** `.floating-cta` is visible from first paint with no scroll-linked reveal, sitting on screen alongside the hero's already-loud "Залишити заявку" button, both pointing to `#contact`. Makes more sense once a visitor has scrolled past the hero and lost sight of the primary button.

**[P3] No skip-to-content link.** Keyboard users must tab through logo + 4 nav links + language toggle before reaching hero content. Low severity given the short nav, but a standard cheap affordance.
